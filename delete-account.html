Account & Data Deletion

Manage your Kicapp account data safely and securely

At Kicapp, we respect your privacy and give you full control over your data. You can request to delete your account and all associated personal data using either of the two methods below.

📱 Method 1: Delete via Kicapp App

If you have the application installed on your device, you can delete your account instantly by following these simple steps:

  • Open the Kicapp application and Log in.
  • Navigate to the More tab.
  • Click on your Profile icon.
  • Scroll down and select Delete Account.
  • Confirm the deletion to permanently erase your data.

📧 Method 2: Request via Email

If you no longer have access to the app, you can request full account deletion by contacting our support team. We will process your request and permanently delete your data within 7 business days.
